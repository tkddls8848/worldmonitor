---
title: "Stock Research"
description: "Open the Stock Research overlay from the Markets panel to see company fundamentals, analyst context, price history, and recent news."
---

Select an equity in the Markets panel to open its Stock Research overlay. A specific symbol can also be opened at `/stocks/<symbol>`.

The overlay combines price history and chart data with the structured response from `GET /api/market/v1/analyze-stock`. It can show fundamentals, analyst targets, earnings context, and recent headlines. The analysis includes its source and availability so missing inputs remain visible.

Earnings context comes from the shared calendar seed, which also serves the Daily Market Brief. The [stock news search layer](/premium-finance-search) describes headline retrieval and fallback behavior.

See [Finance data](/finance-data) for market-data sources and RPCs.
